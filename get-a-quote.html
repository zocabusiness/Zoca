FAQ

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Tell us what your business does, what you want to create, where the project will take place, your ideal timing and any important deliverables.

Do you offer custom packages?

Yes. Every project is different, so we shape the production scope and deliverables around your objectives.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as possible is best, particularly for projects with fixed travel dates or larger production requirements.

Do you travel for projects?

Yes. We are available for projects in Bali and internationally. Travel and production logistics can be included in the proposal.
